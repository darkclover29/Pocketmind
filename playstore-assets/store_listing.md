## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
Ever wish you could use AI without wondering who's reading your conversations? PocketShadow is a completely private AI assistant that runs entirely on your Android phone — no internet connection, no account, no data ever leaving your device. Just you and a genuinely capable AI, offline.

🔒 TRUE PRIVACY — NO INTERNET REQUIRED
Your conversations never leave your device. Unlike cloud AI apps (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.), PocketShadow processes everything locally using Google's Gemma model. There are no servers, no logs, no subscriptions, and nothing to leak.

⚡ FULLY ON-DEVICE AI
PocketShadow runs on Google's LiteRT-LM runtime to execute the Gemma language model directly on your phone's CPU or GPU. Fast, private, and works completely offline — on a plane, in a tunnel, or anywhere without signal.

💬 WHERE PRIVATE AI ACTUALLY MATTERS
• Brainstorm sensitive personal or business ideas without them touching a server
• Draft messages, journal, or think out loud with zero data-retention risk
• Get quick answers on a flight, in the backcountry, or anywhere offline
• Use AI in places with strict data policies — no network call means no compliance gray zone

🎨 BEAUTIFUL, FOCUSED DESIGN
• 5 handcrafted themes: Dark, Navy, Forest, Light, and Warm
• OLED-optimized dark modes for battery savings
• Smooth streaming responses — watch the AI think in real time
• Swipe-to-delete with confirmation — no accidental deletions
• Adjustable AI memory (context window 4–20 messages)

📂 FULL CONVERSATION HISTORY
• Organize chats with automatic titles
• Instantly search and switch between past conversations
• Disable history entirely for one-session privacy

🛡️ DESIGNED FOR PRIVACY BY DEFAULT
• No INTERNET permission — physically incapable of sending data
• No account or sign-in
• No analytics, no tracking, no advertising
• Chat history stored locally only — excluded from Google backup
• Delete everything by clearing app data

⚠️ AI DISCLAIMER
Responses are generated by a local language model and may be inaccurate. Do not use PocketShadow for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions.

📋 NO MANUAL SETUP REQUIRED
The Gemma model is pre-bundled and downloaded automatically with the app from the Play Store. No manual downloads or file transfers are required.

Private AI, no strings attached. Download PocketShadow.
```
