# PocketShadow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PocketShadow is an on-device chat app. PocketShadow does not send your chats,
preferences, or microphone audio to PocketShadow servers. The app has no
Internet permission, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or remote account.

## Data stored on your device

PocketShadow stores app preferences on your device and stores chat history
there only when chat history is enabled in Settings. You can delete individual
chats in the app or clear PocketShadow's data in Android Settings.

## AI processing

Your messages and any attached local document context are processed by the
Gemma model running on your device. Prompts and responses are not sent to
PocketShadow servers.

## Voice input and microphone

Voice input is optional. PocketShadow requests the Android `RECORD_AUDIO`
permission only after you tap the microphone. If permission is granted,
PocketShadow uses Android's on-device speech recognizer to turn speech into
text. The transcription appears in the message composer for you to review.
PocketShadow does not record or save microphone audio. If the device does not
provide an on-device recognizer, or it cannot start, voice dictation is
unavailable; PocketShadow does not fall back to an online recognizer. You can
type your message instead.

## Read aloud

Read-aloud uses an offline text-to-speech voice. If one is not installed,
PocketShadow asks you to install an offline voice in Android settings; it does
not use an online voice.

## Permissions

- `RECORD_AUDIO`: optional voice dictation, requested only when you tap the
  microphone. Audio is processed by the on-device recognizer and is not
  recorded or retained by PocketShadow.
- `VIBRATE`: optional haptic feedback for selected actions.

## Children, tracking, and advertising

PocketShadow contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use
cross-app trackers. It does not collect children's data.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on PocketShadow's
Google Play Store listing.
